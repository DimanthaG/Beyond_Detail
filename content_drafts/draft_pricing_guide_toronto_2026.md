## 1. Window Tinting Cost: Standard vs. Ceramic

Price is often the deciding factor for tint, but not all films are created equal. The biggest price driver is the **technology** of the film.

### Standard (Dyed) Film: $200 - $300
This is the entry-level option. It provides privacy and that sleek "blacked-out" look, but offers minimal heat rejection. It's strictly for aesthetics.

### Nano-Ceramic Film (LLumar/XPEL): $350 - $600+
This is where the real value lies. Ceramic window tint blocks up to 98% of Infrared (IR) heat. It doesn't just look cool; it literally keeps your car cool. For a Tesla Model 3 or SUV with a panoramic roof, this is essential for summer comfort in Toronto.

*Note: Prices vary by vehicle size (Coupe vs. SUV) and complexity (e.g., Tesla rear windows requiring a single-piece install).*

## 2. Ceramic Coating Cost: Why Is It Expensive?

Ceramic coating isn't just a "wax." It's a semi-permanent chemical bond that protects your clear coat. The primary cost factor isn't the bottle of coating—it's the **Paint Correction** required before application.

If we coat over scratches, we lock them in forever. That's why professional packages include polishing steps.

*   **Entry-Level (1-Year Protection): $600 - $800.** Includes light polishing to enhance gloss.
*   **Pro Grade (5-Year Protection): $1,200 - $1,500.** Includes 2-step paint correction to remove 80-90% of swirl marks.
*   **Lifetime Package: $1,800+.** Multi-layer application for maximum hardness and durability.
