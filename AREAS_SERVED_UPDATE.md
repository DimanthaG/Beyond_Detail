# ✅ Visual Polish & SEO - Areas We Serve

## 🎨 Visual Updates
- **Theme Consistency:** Replaced all purple accents (`#8b5cf6`, `#3b82f6`) in the "Areas We Serve" section with the site's signature orange theme (`#f07900`, `#d35100`).
- **Elements Updated:**
  - Icons (Map pin, arrows)
  - Text highlights (Region names, "Scarborough" in footer)
  - Buttons and gradients
  - Hover effects and shadows

## 🔍 SEO & Accessibility Improvements
- **Descriptive Links:** Added `aria-label` attributes to all links in the section.
  - "View Services" -> `aria-label="View auto detailing services in [City Name]"`
  - "Book Your Service" -> `aria-label="Book your auto detailing service"`
- **Benefit:** This helps search engines understand the destination of the links better than generic "View Services" text, improving internal linking value.
