## 📝 Notes

1. **Dynamic Routes:** Routes with `:parameter` are dynamic and depend on content from Sanity CMS
2. **Alternative Routes:** Some pages have multiple routes (e.g., `/contact` and `/contact-us` both go to the same page)
3. **404 Page:** Any unmatched route will show the Error/404 page
4. **All URLs are case-sensitive** and use lowercase with hyphens
