## Are Your Windows Legal in 2026?

Window tinting is one of the best upgrades you can make for your vehicle. It adds privacy, blocks UV rays, and just looks incredible. But in Ontario, **not all tint is created equal**.

As we head into 2026, police in Scarborough and the GTA are stricter than ever about visibility. Before you tint your car, you need to know exactly what is legal to avoid a $110+ ticket and a mandatory removal order.

Here is the definitive guide to **Ontario Window Tint Laws for 2026**.

---

### 1. The "Golden Rule": Front Left & Right Windows
This is where 90% of drivers get in trouble.

**The Law:**
The Highway Traffic Act (HTA) in Ontario states that the front left and right windows must allow the driver to be **clearly seen**. While there isn't a specific percentage written into the *statute* like in some US states, the widely accepted enforcement limit in 2026 is **30% Visible Light Transmission (VLT)**.

*   **Legal:** 30% VLT or lighter (allows 30% of light in).
*   **Risky:** 20% VLT (Factory Match). Some officers may let this slide, but it risks a fine.
*   **Illegal:** 5% VLT (Limo Tint). This is almost guaranteed to get you pulled over on the front windows.

**Our Recommendation for Scarborough Drivers:**
Stick to **35% tint** for your front windows. It provides a sleek, smoky look that cuts glare but keeps you 100% safe from fines.

### 2. The Back Windows (Rear Passengers & Rear Windshield)
Good news: You have freedom here.

**The Law:**
For removing passengers and the rear windshield, **any darkness is legal** in Ontario, provided your vehicle has functional left and right exterior mirrors.

*   **Popular Choice:** 20% (Factory Match) – Matches the stock privacy glass on SUVs.
*   **Privacy Choice:** 5% (Limo Tint) – Complete privacy. People outside cannot see in. Perfect for work trucks or if you carry expensive gear updates.

### 3. The Windshield
**The Law:**
It is **illegal** to tint your entire windshield in Ontario.

*   **Exception:** You *are* allowed to have a tint strip (known as an "eyebrow") on the top 3-6 inches of the windshield. This is excellent for blocking the sun without obstructing your view.
*   **New Trend:** **70% Ceramic Air Blue**. Many tint shops (like us!) offer a virtually clear ceramic film for the windshield. It blocks heat and UV rays without darkening the glass, keeping you cool and legal.
