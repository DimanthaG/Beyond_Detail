## 🧼 Factors That Influence Detailing Costs

Why does one shop charge $150 and another $400? Here are the key factors:

### 1. Vehicle Size
It takes significantly more time and product to detail a Cadillac Escalade than a Honda Civic. Most detailers categorize pricing into two or three tiers: **Small (Sedan/Coupe)**, **Mid-Size (Small SUV)**, and **Large (Truck/Minivan/7-Seater)**.

### 2. Condition of the Vehicle
This is the biggest variable.
*   **Standard Condition:** Normal dust, light dirt, minor crumbs.
*   **Heavy Soiling:** Deep stains, excessive mud, salt stains from Toronto winters.
*   **Pet Hair:** Removing pet hair is labor-intensive and often incurs a surcharge ($50-$100).
*   **Biohazards:** Mold, vomit, or spills require specialized safety equipment and chemicals, costing significantly more.

### 3. Type of Service (Mobile vs. Shop)
*   **In-Shop:** You bring the car to a controlled environment (like our shop at 170 Finchdene Square). This often allows for higher-end services like ceramic coating.
*   **Mobile Detailing:** The detailer comes to your driveway. This offers maximum convenience but might carry a slight premium for travel time and convenience. **Beyond Detail** offers both options to suit your lifestyle.

## 📦 What's Included in Detailing Packages?

Understanding what you get for your money is crucial.

### Entry-Level / Express Detail ($150 - $250)
Perfect for maintenance between deep cleans.
*   Exterior hand wash and dry.
*   Wheel and tire cleaning + tire shine.
*   Interior vacuum (seats, carpets, mats).
*   Wipe down of dashboard and door panels.
*   Interior and exterior glass cleaning.

### Signature / Deep Interior Detail ($250 - $400)
Ideal for families, daily drivers, or selling a car.
*   **Everything in Express, PLUS:**
*   **Steam Cleaning:** To kill bacteria and lift grime from vents/crevices.
*   **Shampoo & Extraction:** Deep cleaning of cloth seats and carpets to remove stains and salt.
*   **Leather Care:** Deep cleaning and conditioning of leather seats.
*   **Salt Removal:** Crucial for Scarborough/GTA winters.

### Exterior Paint Correction & Protection ($400 - $1,000+)
For car enthusiasts who want a mirror-like finish.
*   **Decontamination:** Clay bar treatment to remove embedded iron/brake dust.
*   **Polishing (Paint Correction):** Machine polishing to remove swirl marks and scratches.
*   **Protection:** Application of high-grade sealant or **Ceramic Coating**.

## 💎 Is Ceramic Coating Worth the Extra Cost?

If you see prices in the $1,000+ range, it likely involves **Ceramic Coating**.

Unlike traditional wax that lasts 3 months, a Ceramic Coating (like the ones we install at Beyond Detail) creates a permanent or semi-permanent bond with your paint.
*   **Longevity:** Lasts 2-5 years (or Lifetime with higher packages).
*   **Protection:** Resists UV rays, bird droppings, tree sap, and road salt.
*   **Ease of Cleaning:** Hydrophobic properties make washing your car incredibly easy.

For many Scarborough drivers, protecting their vehicle from road salt and oxidation makes Ceramic Coating a smart long-term investment.
