# 14-Day Local SEO Ranking Strategy Implementation

Based on the "14-Day Ranking Strategy" video, we have implemented the following high-impact changes to your website. This strategy focuses on aligning your website perfectly with your Google Business Profile (GBP) to dominate the "Map Pack".

## ✅ Implemented Changes

### 1. "Perfect Structure" Homepage Content
**Goal:** Tell Google exactly what services you offer in your specific location.
*   **Action:** We restructured the "Our Services" section on the Homepage.
*   **Change:** Instead of a generic list, we now have specific H2 headings for each major service category, explicitly mentioning "Scarborough" and "Toronto".
    *   `<h2>Auto Detailing in Scarborough</h2>`
    *   `<h2>Window Tinting Service in Scarborough</h2>`
    *   `<h2>Ceramic Coating & Paint Correction</h2>`
*   **Why:** This mirrors the "Secondary Categories" advice from the script, building strong local relevance.

### 2. Google Map Embed (Trust Signal)
**Goal:** Verify your physical location to Google.
*   **Action:** Added a Google Maps embed directly to the Contact section.
*   **Why:** The script identifies this as a critical "Technical Trust Signal" that connects your website entity to your physical GMB location.

### 3. Descriptive Link Text (SEO)
**Goal:** Help Google understand what your pages are about.
*   **Action:** Updated generic "Learn More" buttons to be specific (e.g., "Learn More about Window Tinting").
*   **Why:** This passes more relevance to your internal pages, boosting their ranking potential.

## 🚀 Next Steps (For You)

To fully execute the 14-day strategy, here are the remaining steps you should take:

### 1. Google Business Profile (GBP) Optimization
*   **Add Categories:** Ensure you have 4-5 relevant categories in your GMB profile (e.g., Car Detailing Service, Window Tinting Service, Auto Painting, Car Wash).
*   **Add Services:** Add 30+ specific services to your GMB profile (e.g., "Tesla Window Tinting", "Interior Shampoo", "Clay Bar Treatment").

### 2. Get "Verification" Backlinks (Critical)
The script emphasizes that **External Links** are the final piece of the puzzle.
*   **Action:**
    *   **Chamber of Commerce:** Join the Scarborough or Toronto Chamber of Commerce and get a link.
    *   **Sponsorships:** Sponsor a local youth sports team (baseball, soccer) for a link.
    *   **Citations:** Ensure your business is listed on YellowPages, Yelp, etc., with the **EXACT** same Name, Address, and Phone Number (NAP).

### 3. Expand Service Pages (Long-Term)
*   **Action:** Create specific pages for your most profitable services.
*   **Example:** Instead of just one `/tint` page, consider adding content specifically for "Ceramic Window Tinting" or "Carbon Window Tinting".
