## 📝 **How It Works**

When you write blog content in Sanity, the system automatically:

1. **Scans the text** for service and location keywords
2. **Creates internal links** to relevant pages
3. **Prioritizes combinations** (e.g., "window tint scarborough" over just "window tint")
4. **Limits links** to prevent over-optimization
5. **Maintains styling** with hover effects

**Example:**
```
Your text: "Looking for window tint in Scarborough? Our ceramic coating services..."

Becomes: "Looking for [window tint in Scarborough]? Our [ceramic coating] services..."
```
(Where brackets indicate clickable links)
